## A. Status & credentials

- **Howard Marks**: co-founder (1995) and **Co-Chairman** of Oaktree Capital Management. He is also a Brookfield Corporation director and chair of Brookfield's Investment Solutions Group. Source (deal-completion report, 2026-07-30/31): https://www.marketscreener.com/news/brookfield-corporation-tsx-bn-and-brookfield-asset-management-ltd-completed-the-acquisition-of-th-ce7e5fdbdd88f624
- **Bruce Karsh**: Co-Chairman and **Chief Investment Officer**. He is PM for Global Opportunities (distressed/"Opps") and Global Credit, and was expected to join the BAM board at or before closing. Source (BOH 8-K ex.99.1, 2025-10-13): https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403528/000119312525237852/d23281dex991.htm
- **Who runs Oaktree day to day.** Co-CEOs **Robert (Bob) O'Leary** (PM, Global Opportunities) and **Armen Panossian** (head of Performing Credit) took over from Jay Wintrob in Q1 2024. This was announced in June 2023. Todd Molz was named COO at that time; the FY2024 10-K later calls him "former General Counsel and Chief Administrative Officer." Source: https://www.opalesque.com/698004/Oaktree_changes_leadership_prepares_for_new_landscape800.html (2023-06-29). Bob O'Leary is quoted as Co-CEO in the Feb 2025 Opps XII release: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250210248809/en
- **Ownership**
  - Brookfield bought about 61–62% of Oaktree in 2019.
  - **2025-10-13:** Brookfield agreed to buy the remaining **~26%** for **~$3.0bn** (BAM ~$1.6bn, BN ~$1.4bn). Holders could take cash, BAM shares (2-year lock-up) or BN shares (5-year lock-up). Expected close was Q1 2026. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403528/000119312525237852/d23281dex991.htm
  - **2026-03-05:** European Commission approval (per MarketScreener).
  - **2026-04-14:** Transaction Agreement signed.
  - **Closed 2026-07-31**, per BOH 8-K Item 2.01. Marks, Karsh, John Frank, Sheldon Stone, Bruce Flatt and others resigned from the BOH board, and Brookfield finance staff replaced them. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403528/000140352826000068/oak-20260731.htm
  - BAM's Q3 2026 release (2026-11-06) confirms completion in July 2026 and books a **$2.0bn non-cash gain** on acquiring and consolidating Oaktree. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001937926/000117184326007210/exh_991.htm
