**Strategy and targets ("& INNOVATION 2030", announced Apr-2024)**
- Targets "around FY2030": EPS growth +8% or more per year (CAGR from the FY2023 base of ¥78.5) and ROE 10% or more. Source: 2Q deck, Nov 2026, https://www.mitsuifudosan.co.jp/english/corporate/ir/presentation/pdf/FR_BH2611e.pdf [P]
- Targets for the FY ending Mar-2027 under the plan: business income ¥440bn or more, net profit ¥270bn or more, ROE 8.5% or more, ROA 5% or more, total assets about ¥9tn, interest-bearing debt about ¥4.5tn, D/E 1.2-1.5x. Same source [P].
- Plan versus guidance for FY Mar-2027: business income ¥450bn, net profit ¥285bn, interest-bearing debt ¥4.8tn (above the plan figure of about ¥4.5tn), total assets ¥10.1tn at Mar-2026. Same source [P].
- Results: ROE 8.0% (FY Mar-2025) and 8.7% (FY Mar-2026). Business income ¥398.6bn and ¥445.1bn. Net profit ¥248.7bn and ¥278.6bn. EPS growth CAGR is about +13.4%/yr. Same source [P].
- "Asset turnover with no exceptions": fixed assets and real property for sale are treated as one pool, about ¥2tn per period. Same source [P].
- Cash allocation: about ¥2.2tn each of inflows and outflows achieved over the first two years, about 80% of the three-year plan. Asset-turnover proceeds were about ¥1,240bn (about 60% of plan). Example sales: Otemachi One Tower, LaLaport Aichi Togo and Yokohama Mitsui Building (FY Mar-2025); Mita Garden Hills, former Hibiya U-1 Building, US rental housing and DiverCity Tokyo Plaza (FY Mar-2026). Same source [P].
- Strategic shareholdings: cumulative reduction of about 40% by the end of FY Mar-2026 against a 50% goal. Targeted "50% or higher" by the end of FY Mar-2027, and reduction continues. Same source [P].
