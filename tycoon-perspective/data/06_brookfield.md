- Brookfield Corporation (BN) is the parent.
- Brookfield Asset Management (BAM) is the listed asset manager. Asset Management AUM was "over $1 trillion" at Mar 31 and Jun 30, 2026 (BN Q1 and Q2 2026 MD&A). BAM's Q4-25 supplemental headlines $1.2T; the segment sum is ~$1,181B.
