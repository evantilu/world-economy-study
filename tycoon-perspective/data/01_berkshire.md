## A. Status & credentials

**Leadership timeline (all from primary releases)**
- **2025-05-03 (AGM):** Buffett said he would recommend Greg Abel as CEO effective 2026-01-01.
- **2025-05-04:** The board voted unanimously to appoint Abel President & CEO effective 2026-01-01. Buffett was to remain Chairman. Source: https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/news/may0525.pdf
- **2025-11-06 "It's Not Me" release:** Warned about AI-generated deepfake videos of Buffett. Stated that "Mr. Buffett has rarely commented publicly since Berkshire's shareholder's meeting last May." Source: https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/news/nov0625.pdf
- **2025-11-10 Thanksgiving letter:** Buffett wrote, "I will no longer be writing Berkshire's annual report or talking endlessly at the annual meeting. As the British would say, I'm 'going quiet.' Sort of." He said he would keep talking to shareholders through an annual Thanksgiving message. The same day he converted 1,800 A shares into 2,700,000 B shares and gave them to four family foundations. [THX-25]
- **2025-12-08 leadership changes:**
  - Todd Combs "will conclude his tenure at Berkshire Hathaway and join JPMorgan Chase & Co." Combs had been GEICO CEO since 2020 and a JPMorgan director since 2016.
  - Nancy Pierce became GEICO CEO.
  - Adam Johnson (NetJets CEO) was named President of Consumer Products, Service and Retailing.
  - CFO Marc Hamburg will retire 2027-06-01 and Charles C. Chang will succeed him.
  - Buffett's quote: "Todd A. Combs … has resigned to accept an interesting and important job at JPMorgan … JPMorgan, as usually is the case, has made a good decision."
  - Source: https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/news/dec0825.pdf
- **2026-01-01:** Abel became President & CEO. The 2025 10-K describes the repurchase program as now run by "Berkshire's Chief Executive Officer, after consultation with the Chairman of the Board." [10K-25]
- **2026-02-28:** Abel published his first annual letter [LTR-25]. Content:
  - "Ted Weschler manages about 6% of our investments, including a portion of the portfolio formerly overseen by Todd Combs."
  - "At Berkshire, equity investments are fundamental to our capital allocation activities; responsibility ultimately resides with me as CEO."
  - Buffett is "in the office five days a week."
  - [R2] On 2026-11-08 I re-downloaded https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2025ltr.pdf and text-matched every [LTR-25] quote in sections A, B and C. Every quote appears verbatim.
    - The numbers in the core-holdings and trading-house tables also match the PDF: cost, market value, dividends, % owned, $194bn, $297.8bn, $2.5bn, $24.5bn, 1.2%, 5.75 years, 34.5%, $230M, $15.382bn, $35.368bn, $862M.
    - The letter contains no mention of China, Taiwan, tariffs, gold, crypto/Bitcoin, private credit or the federal deficit. "dollar" appears only in the dividend-policy sentence.
- **2026-05-02 AGM (Abel's first):** Abel gave a business update, then two Q&A sessions: Abel with Ajit Jain, then Abel with Adam Johnson and Katie Farmer (BNSF). The meeting was webcast on CNBC. Source: https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/news/apr2926.pdf
  - Secondary reports say Buffett (95) sat in the audience and made only brief remarks. Source: Fortune https://www.fortune.com/2026/05/04/annual-meeting-berkshire-hathaway-warren-buffett-greg-abel-charlie-munger/ and https://www.itiger.com/news/1133210139
  - [R2] CNBC confirms Buffett sat in the front row of the directors' section and spoke from his seat. His words: "We couldn't have made a better decision. that's been 100% successful. [He's doing] everything I did and then some. He's the right person." He also gave a separate sideline interview to Becky Quick during the lunch break. [CNBC-26-LIVE], [CNBC-26-WEB]
  - [R2] CNBC reported that Abel said he used his entire after-tax salary of $15 million to buy Berkshire shares personally, and plans to do so every year he is CEO. This is CNBC's paraphrase, not a direct quote. [CNBC-26-LIVE]
  - [R2] In the 2026-05-01 Squawk Box interview, Abel said a 20-year tenure "would not surprise me, but that's up to our board." [CNBC-26-AGA]
- **2026-07-14:** Buffett converted 8,000 A shares into 12,000,000 B shares for donation to four foundations. His remaining holding is 188,290 A shares and 1,162 B shares. Quote: "My goal is to dispose of all of my Berkshire shares within about eight years … by December 31, 2034." Source: https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/news/jul1426.pdf
- **2026-09-18:** **Buffett became Chairman Emeritus and remains a director. Howard G. Buffett was elected Chairman.** Susan Decker remains Lead Independent Director. Source: https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/news/sep1826.pdf
  - Buffett's letter: "He [Greg] has been making the decisions that matter for some time now, and I have not had to think twice about any of them … Greg runs the company; Howard will guard its culture and values."
- **Ted Weschler:** Still at Berkshire per the Feb 2026 letter (manages about 6% of investments, has a "broader role in assessing significant opportunities"). I found no later change in primary sources.

**Credentials:** Berkshire market-value gains vs. the S&P 500 since 1965 are tabulated in each annual letter [LTR-25].
- Insurance float was about $176bn at YE2025 [ER-25Q4] and about $178.5bn at 2026-09-30 [ER-26Q3].
- Operating earnings: $44.5bn in 2025, $47.4bn in 2024 [LTR-25, ER-25Q4].
